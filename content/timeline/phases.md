title: Areas of development
---

These phases are an editorial grouping, not a measured chronology.

- Foundation: Linux and Proxmox provide a virtualized environment for services.
- Services: Podman, Quadlet and systemd manage containerized applications.
- Automation and observability: Ansible, Semaphore, Grafana, Loki and telemetry make operations more inspectable.
- Accelerated computing: a GPU lifecycle controller coordinates isolated lab work and inference.
