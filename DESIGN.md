# Design

Experience mode: an evidence-led portfolio, read first and explored optionally.

Atlas uses a light neutral ground (#f4f6f5), deep ink (#152129) and blue (#2459b7). The Observatory uses a dark blue-grey ground (#101c27), light text (#eef4f6) and the same blue for relationships and selection. Semantic control colors preserve contrast. One local open-license sans spans both surfaces.

The first viewport pairs a concise account of the work with an authored infrastructure atlas: a stylized server connects the themes of compute, services and observation against a light technical grid. The main reading column stays left aligned. The Observatory moves from milestone controls to an illustrative scene and an accessible topology, then to contextual case links. Equipment depicts roles rather than a real installation. A 2D representation carries the same meaning without WebGL or on mobile.

Motion responds to navigation and selection only; reduced motion removes camera transitions. Focus is visible, content is available outside the canvas, and controls remain reachable by keyboard and touch.
